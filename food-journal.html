    The routine

    Five rules for the camp kitchen

    1. 1Sunday planning, 15 minutes. Pick the week's dinners from the log and write one grocery list.
    2. 2One supply run. Shop once from the list so there are no midweek trips that end at a drive-through.
    3. 3Cook once, eat twice. At least one batch night a week, with extras packed for lunch.
    4. 4Match the calendar. Save the quick recipes for exam weeks and late practices.
    5. 5Rate every dish. Keeper, tweak or drop. Keepers go into the rotation.
    Build the plate

    The Mediterranean plate

    • Half: vegetables
    • A quarter: chicken, fish or turkey
    • A quarter: beans, lentils or whole grains

    Olive oil is the main fat. Still hungry after a long practice? Add brown rice or a whole-wheat pita on the side.
