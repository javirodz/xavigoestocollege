The Three Dig Sites

The school's own deadline and requirements for each site. Early Action is non-binding at all three.

Site 1

Virginia Tech

Submitted Oct 4, 2026. Official deadline was Nov 15.

  • Ut Prosim Profile: 4 short answers, 120 words each, now locked
  • Honors College box checked
  • Next: the Applicant Portal email, then the self-reported STARS course record
  • Test optional
  • Fee $60
Site 2

James Madison

Nov 1. Every piece is due that day.

  • Application, official transcript with senior schedule, and Honors essays all due Nov 1
  • Honors College: 2 essays, 200 words each (the prompts say max 220), entered with the application
  • Transcript sent electronically by the school counselor
  • Test optional
  • Fee $70
Site 3

George Mason

Nov 1, 11:59 pm ET. Regular Decision is Feb 1.

  • Honors College essay: 500 words max, choose 1 of 2 prompts
  • Official transcript required
  • Recommendation letter submitted through the Common App
  • Score optional
  • Fee $75
Plan: Virginia Tech went in on October 4. The target for James Madison and George Mason is Thursday, October 29, which leaves three days of cushion before the Sunday, November 1 deadline.
