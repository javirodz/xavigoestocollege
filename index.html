Field Skills: How to Study Math

Grades still count while the applications are out. This is the study method for math, written for any math class, not just Calculus.

Field notes

Nobody taught me how to study math

Dad got two Cs on his first two college Calculus exams. Then he went to a seminar on how to study for math, changed how he studied, and got two As on the next two. The guide puts those rules on one page.

Math is not something you read. You learn it by doing it, with a pencil, a little bit every day.
Read the blog post Print the one-pager (PDF)
The core rules
  • 1Sit down with it every day, 30 to 60 minutes.
  • 2Understand the idea first, then practice.
  • 4Pencil and paper. Write every step.
  • 6Keep a mistake log and redo those problems.
  • 7Quiz yourself. Do not just reread.
  • AITry it on paper first. Use the AI tutor to check, not to solve.
